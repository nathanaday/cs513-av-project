# CSCI 513 Project Registration Instructions

Category: group project. This page belongs to [[cs513-project]], not to the course material.

## Summary

A two-page Word document from the course, file name `CSCI 513 Project Teams.docx`. Page 1 tells each team how to register its project. Page 2 gives a worked example. This is the only course guidance on the pre-proposal submission that the knowledge base holds.

## Key claims

Requirements (page 1):

- Each team submits one page. The source says "please stick to one page".
- The page must contain:
  - the project title;
  - the cluster the project belongs to, C1 to C8. The source points to `1-CourseIntro.pttx`, slide 13, for the cluster list. That deck is not in the knowledge base;
  - the names of the team members;
  - an abstract of 5 to 6 lines;
  - the key project milestones.

Example (page 2):

- Title: "Mobile Drone-base and Drone-Team for package delivery".
- Team members: the example shows a placeholder for 5 to 6 names.
- Abstract: one paragraph. It states the system, the algorithms the team will develop, the main challenge, and the evaluation: simulation scenarios with the metrics delivery time, energy consumption, and fleet utilization.
- Figure 1: a concept of operations image. It shows a delivery van with a drone launch deck on a residential street and drones that carry packages to houses.
- Milestones: a Gantt-style table. Rows: System Design, Base Planning, Drone Coordination, System Integration, Testing & Evaluation, Final Presentation, Final Report. Columns: Sep 1–15, Sep 16–Oct 5, Oct 6–25, Oct 26–Nov 15, Nov 16–Dec 1, Dec 2–10, and a Lead column that assigns each row to a member (M1 to M5). The table marks the Final Presentation on Dec 1 and the Final Report on Dec 10.
- The source tells teams to make their own milestones "with appropriate titles".
- The source labels the example abstract and milestone table as written with ChatGPT, and the figure as AI-generated.

## Notes

- Synthesis: the example implies the expected format. It has a short abstract that names the evaluation metrics, one concept figure, and a milestone table with a lead for each row. The source does not say that the figure or the Lead column is required.
- The source gives no due date for the registration page. [[CS513 Project Proposal Draft]] lists "Project pitch due" on Sep 21. No source in the knowledge base says that the pitch and the registration page are the same submission.
- The Dec 1 and Dec 10 dates in the example agree with the dates in [[CS513 Project Proposal Draft]].
